# Astana 3D: open data and self-hosted geometry

Verified 19 October 2026. Research only; no application or map-rendering code changed.

## Finding

No verified, openly licensed, ready-to-stream Astana city dataset meeting the requested architectural accuracy was found. Open-source software can render substantially richer shapes than footprint extrusions, but the current Astana source data do not provide complete, accurate architecture. In particular, changing from OpenFreeMap extrusions to an OSM-aware roof renderer does **not** establish an accurate Bayterek or alem.ai model.

The most credible reusable open pipeline is **OSM2World → tiled GLB / 3D Tiles**, including automatic lookup of community models through `3dmr` tags. It is viable for a procedural city and source-backed parts/roofs. It does not meet the requirement for a verified accurate whole city without additional city geometry. Its software capability and its Astana data coverage must be evaluated separately. [OSM2World capabilities](https://osm2world.org/), [3DMR integration](https://osm2world.org/blog/2025/09/05/3dmr/).

## Direct tests of current Astana data

Small read-only requests inspected five landmark areas through Overpass and the main OSM API. Overpass returned repeated 500 errors on several filtered queries; successful unfiltered local queries and bounded OSM map downloads supplied the evidence. The exact requests, statuses, tags and timestamps are retained in [OSM evidence](evidence/astana-osm-landmark-tags.json). No OSM data were edited.

The sphere discrepancy has independent primary evidence: the [architect's project description](https://www.smithgill.com/work/kazakhstan_pavilion_science_museum/) describes an 80 m diameter, 100 m high modified sphere with a carved inlet for wind turbines. [The owner](https://qazexpocongress.kz/en/nur-alem/) confirms both dimensions and that the building now houses alem.ai. [Khan Shatyr's architect](https://www.fosterandpartners.com/projects/khan-shatyr-entertainment-center/) describes its geometry and structure.

The OSM2World source was checked at commit `f6518b35117cf7864e9c6f68c3b303a006e27b8f` dated 9 September 2026: [roof dispatcher](https://github.com/tordanik/OSM2World/blob/f6518b35117cf7864e9c6f68c3b303a006e27b8f/core/src/main/java/org/osm2world/world/modules/building/roof/Roof.java#L113), [ridge-based round roof](https://github.com/tordanik/OSM2World/blob/f6518b35117cf7864e9c6f68c3b303a006e27b8f/core/src/main/java/org/osm2world/world/modules/building/roof/RoundRoof.java#L22). The dispatcher supports pyramidal, dome, cone, round, onion and several other roof families. It cannot recover absent architectural geometry.

A separate convenience sample around Bayterek, bbox `[71.427, 51.126, 71.434, 51.1305]`, contained **22 building/part elements**: 2 had explicit heights, 17 had levels, 5 had neither; 20 lacked a roof shape, one used `orb`, one `round`, and none had `3dmr`. These are OSM elements, not 22 deduplicated independent buildings. This small central sample is not a citywide completeness estimate. It does demonstrate that a pipeline must either infer dimensions or omit some depth even here.

## Candidate assessment

| Candidate | Geometry, coverage and access | Assessment for this requirement |

## Terrain, imagery and licensing

Terrain is independently solvable with public data. An unauthenticated HEAD request returned 200 for an Astana [Terrarium tile](https://s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prod/terrarium/12/2860/1368.png) and a [Copernicus GLO-30 tile covering 51–52° N, 71–72° E](https://copernicus-dem-30m.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N51_00_E071_00_DEM/Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N51_00_E071_00_DEM.tif). These establish access, not a surveyed precision assessment. [Terrain Tiles](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/) provides terrain elevation; [Copernicus DEM](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/) is a surface model including vegetation/infrastructure at nominal 30 m, not individual architectural geometry.

Preserve the appropriate [Terrain Tiles source attributions](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md), and comply with the Copernicus product licence. OSM data requires ODbL attribution and applicable database obligations; self-hosting does not remove those. [OSM copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). OSM2World software is MIT-licensed; style assets and external 3DMR models retain their respective licences. Model 4's page, for example, specifies CC0. [Software licence](https://github.com/tordanik/OSM2World/blob/f6518b35117cf7864e9c6f68c3b303a006e27b8f/LICENSE.txt), [model licence example](https://3dmr.eu/model/4).

Open Sentinel-2 imagery has 10 m visible bands and free systematic access. That can cover a terrain surface, but cannot supply detailed building façades or recover Astana's architecture. [Copernicus mission data](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/sentinel-data/sentinel-2). No verified open, current, citywide sub-metre Astana orthophoto licence or photogrammetric mesh was established in this workstream. Public visibility of municipal imagery or commercial tiles alone does not grant redistribution rights.

## Performance and integration implications

A city should be generated and served in spatial tiles with levels of detail. OSM2World can produce real meshes and supports browser-side conversion, but its own [web-library documentation](https://osm2world.org/docs/library-web/) limits that approach to smaller datasets due to performance. For a city, pre-generation, local caching and LOD-based streaming are the sensible implementation direction; no Astana performance benchmark was run.

Terrain alignment is a separate engineering task: roads, water, entrances and tile boundaries must fit the ground. The [June 2026 OSM2World terrain roadmap](https://osm2world.org/blog/2026/06/03/ptf-roadmap-2026-osm-3d-terrain/) explicitly discusses these challenges. Do not infer that a global DEM automatically yields a correctly aligned city.

Keeping POIs, routes, filtering, selection and other existing application features is an integration acceptance condition. Neither replacing the renderer nor importing GLB proves that these features remain correct. A future implementation would need coordinate alignment, depth-aware overlays/picking, loading behaviour and mobile performance checks, plus a 2D mode that hides **all** depth content. This research authorizes no return to generic extrusions or one-by-one handmade landmark patches.

**Decision:** Do not label any inspected open option a perfect or verified accurate Astana 3D map. An OSM2World prototype could demonstrate a richer procedural map, but it would fail the present geometry requirement at known landmarks. A provider or municipal city mesh with explicit reuse terms and verified landmark coverage remains the required input for that requirement.

## Search accounting and limits

This workstream ran 13 Exa searches: **112 requested result slots, 112 returned entries, 95 unique search-result URLs** before deduplication. These are search accounting figures, not 112 independently validated primary sources. The [search log](evidence/astana-open-data-search-log.json) records returned URLs per query. Promising primary documentation was fetched, current source code and small public APIs were inspected, and the Sketchfab preview was visually reviewed. No application performance test, full-city OSM download, Overture geographic scan, authenticated provider call, model purchase or third-party contact was performed.
