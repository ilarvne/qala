Source audit notes — paraphrased observations, not reproduced page contents
Research date: 19 October 2026. See product-research.md for the full decision matrix.

Wanderlog
https://wanderlog.com/trip-planner-ai
Reviewed AI trip planning, suggested activities, optimized itineraries and group planning. These are established competitor features. Qala should not claim an AI itinerary or a map by itself is novel.

Google Maps Ask Maps
https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/ask-maps-immersive-navigation/
Reviewed conversational local discovery and personalized recommendations, including practical questions about local amenities. Qala's narrower differentiator is inspectable Astana evidence and explicit missing-data behavior.

Mindtrip
https://mindtrip.ai/
Reviewed preference-based travel planning and discovery. Persistent preferences are a competitor capability, so Qala's memory story should focus on explicit consent, coarse location and deletion semantics.

VoiceMap
https://voicemap.me/walking-tour-app
Reviewed location-aware audio tours and offline use. Qala should distinguish responsive recommendation planning from prerecorded or authored destination storytelling.

izi.TRAVEL
https://www.izi.travel/en/app
Reviewed multilingual audio guides, museum content and location/QR discovery. Audio narration and language switching are established features rather than exclusive differentiators.

National Museum of the Republic of Kazakhstan
https://www.nmrk.kz/en/
Reviewed the operator's visitor schedule: Tuesday–Sunday 10:00–18:00, Monday closed. The automated enrichment checks this observation before adding it to the POI record and retains a separate evidence URL and timestamp.

Baiterek operator
https://a-vm.kz/en/objects/bayterek/
Reviewed seasonal visitor schedules: June–August 10:00–22:00, otherwise 10:00–21:00. The planner selects a schedule by the planned arrival month and checks the full visit interval.

ICO geolocation guidance
https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/age-appropriate-design-a-code-of-practice-for-online-services/10-geolocation/
Reviewed location granularity, transparent use and protective defaults as privacy-design guidance. This UK source is not evidence of compliance with Kazakhstan law. Qala retains only consented coarse location in preferences and keeps exact planning coordinates transient.
